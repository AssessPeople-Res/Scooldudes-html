Get, Set, Go - Outbound Camp for Life Skills


•Get ready for a rustic wilderness experience filled with everything from basic camping skills to high energy outdoor activities! Yes, come to Get, Set..Go at scooldudes.com, in Nilgiris, where self discovery is an everyday experience.


•At Camp Discovery, students get back to nature, camping in two or three person tents which they learn to pitch themselves, and preparing meals as a group on camp stoves or over the fire at their own campsite within the Camp property.


•Teamwork, cooperation, responsibility and peer support are just a few of the practical life skills our camps help to foster. In addition to the basic camping skills listed below, students will get to spend 2-3 hours each day trying outdoor or adventure activities such as rock climbing, mountain biking, archery, survival skills, rappelling, and Jung,e trails. Towards the end of the camp they have overcome their fears and are ready to set personal goals to conquer.


•Although we follow a different schedule than the traditional resident camps, all students remain connected to each other by participating in all individual and team activities.
