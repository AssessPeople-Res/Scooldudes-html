J4M - Visual Expression Contest

Instructions
Please read these instructions carefully. If you are not clear , we suggest you read it again...reading and re-reading the instructions is not timed....
  • This J4M contest measures your writing ability (visual expression). You will be given 4 minutes to write on a given visual.

  • Once you complete reading these instructions, or re-reading them you can click on the button provided down below on this screen.

  • Once you click on the button you will be displayed a visual. You need to write about the visual for 4 minutes.

  • Once the visual appears, you will be given 2 minutes in order to help you to get your thoughts together and to compose your thoughts.

  • During this period you can see the timer on the right hand top corner of the screen. The timer display will be in seconds and it be displaying the time left in seconds....so it will be starting from 2 minutes and will keep decreasing till it reaches zero second.

  • There will be a button that will be flashing at the bottom of the screen while you are thinking about the visual.

  • You can click on the button to commence writing on the visual if you have finished thinking before the allotted time of 2 minutes. A TEXT BOX will appear for you to start writing about the visual. Else, the TEXT box for your to write will appear automatically once your allotted time for thinking has elapsed.

  • The visual would appear on top of the screen above the TEXT BOX once you start writing and will remain there till you finish writing.

  • Once the TEXT BOX appears on your screen, you can see the timer on the right hand top corner of the screen. Your allotted time is 4 minutes.

  • Once you have reached the allotted time limit, a message will appear on the screen indicating that your time is over.

  • In case you finish writing before the expiry of four minutes you can click on the button provided below the TEXT BOX.

  • There will be no restriction on the number of words you can write...in fact the more you write the better for you.

  • Note : In case of disconnection of your internet connection when you are logged in, please re login with the same User ID and Password to resume.

  • In case you want to go through the instructions again you may do so.....reading and re-reading the instructions is not timed....
Good luck and enjoy the J4M contest
