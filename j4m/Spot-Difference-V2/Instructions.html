J4M - Eagle Eye Contest
Instructions
Please read these instructions carefully. If you are not clear , we suggest you read it again...reading and re-reading the instructions is not timed....
  • This edition of J4M is a "Eagle Eye Contest " contest.

  • You will be given 4 minutes to spot the ten diffences in the two pictures that will be displayed to you.

  • Once you complete reading these instructions, or re-reading them you can click on the button provided down below on this screen.

  • Once you click on the button you will be displayed two visuals with ten Text Boxes for you to type the differences.

  • From the time the contest starts, you will be able to see a timer on the right hand side of the screen. The timer display will be in seconds and it will display the time left in seconds....so it will start from 4 minutes and will keep decreasing till it reaches zero.

  • Once you have reached the allotted time limit, a message will appear on the screen indicating that your time is over.

  • In case you finish writing before the expiry of four minutes you can click on the button provided below the TEXT BOXES.

  • Please keep the points brief ( max 100 characters)

  • Note : In case of disconnection of internet when you are logged in, please re login with the same User ID and Password to resume.

  • In case you want to go through the instructions again you may do so.....reading and re-reading the instructions is not timed....
Good luck and enjoy the J4M contest
