SCOOLDUDES PREMIER LEAGUE(SPL)

(An inter school limited over cricket tournament)

Coming soon dudes ...watch this space....and pad up and roll your sleeves for a cracker of a tournament in the second half of June 2015.

Our aim is to provide dudes from 12 to 18 years of age, from a school, a platform to showcase their talent in cricket and also compete with the other city and district teams of their respective States.

SPL will have the recognition and support of the respective State cricket associations.

In the first phase of SPL, It is proposed to he held in the states of Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala and Andhra Pradesh during the months of June , July and August 2015.

SPL will be organised as a limited over format tournament.

SPL will be managed by a tournament committee comprising senior professionals from corporates with rich experience in leadership positions in addition to key personnel of the respective state cricket associations.

All matches will be conducted in reputed venues used for the league tournaments of the respective state cricket associations.
