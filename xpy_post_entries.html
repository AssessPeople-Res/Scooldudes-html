XPY - Post Entries


Posts in the XPY section will be accepted in the following categories....

  • Articles

  • Cool Quotes

  • Greeting Cards

  • Jokes

Please mail us your entries to buzz@scooldudes.com. Our team will review your entry and on the approval, your post will appear under the appropriate category in the XPY View Entries Section.

XPY Post Guidelines

  • All posts will have to be in English.

  • All posts should be from any one of the above categories.

  • There is no restriction on the number of posts that a person can submit on XPY….the more the merrier!!..

  • On submitting the post, the post will be forwarded to the moderator …..on approval the post will appear under the appropriate category in the XPY section..

  • All approved posts will be eligible for credit points.

  • The decision of the webmaster and moderator on the approval of posts will be final.
